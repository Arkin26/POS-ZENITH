# POS Software

**Built by Zenith Media**

A modern, browser-based Point of Sale system designed for retail businesses that want speed at the counter and clarity in the back office. This POS is one of the software products in the Zenith Media suite — built to be fast, clean, and easy for staff to use from day one.

---

## What it does

### Dashboard
Get a real-time snapshot of your store at a glance.

- **Today's revenue** with day-over-day comparison
- **Order count** and **average order value**
- **Low-stock alerts** so you know what needs reordering
- **Recent orders** table with payment method and totals
- **7-day revenue chart** for quick trend spotting

---

### Point of Sale
A streamlined checkout experience built for busy counters.

- **Product grid** with search and category filters
- **Live stock status** on every product (in stock, low, out of stock)
- **Cart management** — add, remove, adjust quantities, or clear the cart
- **Customer linking** — attach a customer to any sale
- **Discounts** — apply flat discounts before checkout
- **Automatic tax calculation** per product tax rate
- **Multi-payment checkout** — Cash, Card, UPI, and Shopify
- **Change calculation** for cash payments
- **Stock deduction** on completed sales

---

### Receipts
Every transaction, stored and printable.

- **Searchable receipt history** with date, cashier, and amount
- **Full receipt detail** — line items, tax breakdown (CGST/SGST), discounts, and payment info
- **Print-ready receipts** formatted for thermal or standard printers
- **Receipt numbers** generated automatically for every order

---

### Inventory
Full product and stock control in one place.

- **Add, edit, and remove products** (name, SKU, price, category, tax rate)
- **Quick stock adjustments** with +/- controls
- **Stock status badges** — in stock, low stock, out of stock
- **Search** by product name or SKU
- **Categories** — Electronics, Groceries, Clothing, Stationery, Health, Accessories, and more

---

### Reports
Understand how your business is performing over time.

- **Date range filters** — Today, Last 7 days, Last 30 days
- **Revenue, order count, and average order** summaries
- **Top payment method** breakdown
- **Revenue trend line chart**
- **Sales by category bar chart**
- **Recent transactions** table

---

### Customers
Build and manage your customer base.

- **Customer profiles** with name, phone, and email
- **Loyalty points** and **lifetime spend** tracking
- **Add, edit, and remove customers**
- **Purchase history** per customer
- **Link customers to sales** at checkout

---

### Staff Management
Control who works the register and what they can do.

- **Role-based access** — Cashier, Manager, Admin
- **PIN-based staff accounts**
- **Active / inactive status** for each employee
- **Switch active cashier** from the sidebar
- **Add, edit, and remove staff members**

| Role    | Access                          |
|---------|----------------------------------|
| Cashier | Process sales                    |
| Manager | Discounts and refunds            |
| Admin   | Full access                      |

---
